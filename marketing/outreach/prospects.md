# Prospect list sketch — ABQ + Rio Rancho property managers

**Status:** Starter list for manual, lawful outreach. Not an email-ready send file.  
**Rule:** Do not scrape listing sites. Do not harvest personal inboxes. Use a company `info@` / `contact@` / published office email **only if it appears on that company’s own website**. Otherwise keep placeholders and collect the address from a contact form, phone call, or opt-in.

Tokens used below:

- `[PM_NAME]` — leasing manager, broker, or owner you have a lawful reason to contact
- `[COMPANY]` — legal or DBA name
- `[COMPANY_EMAIL]` — fill only from the company’s own site or a documented opt-in
- `[ROLE]` — e.g. Property Manager, Leasing Director, Qualifying Broker

## How to grow this list legally

1. Start from this table (public operators already advertising in the metro).
2. Open **their website** → Contact / About / Footer. Record only a generic office email or “use contact form.”
3. Use AANM public materials and events ([aanm.org](https://www.aanm.org/), public magazine PDFs) to discover more firm names — then repeat step 2. Do not copy member email columns into a blast tool.
4. NARPM New Mexico, Greater Albuquerque Association of REALTORS® property-management sections, and city business registrations are additional **name** sources.
5. Walk or call published office numbers. Ask for the right leasing/operations contact and permission to email.
6. For national operators (Greystar, Bryten, Asset Living, AMC), prefer a **named partnership conversation** over a cold blast to a community `leasing@` box.

## Send-file columns (when you have lawful contacts)

```
company,pm_name,role,market,property_types,website,email,lawful_basis,source_url,date_captured,do_not_contact,notes
```

`lawful_basis` must be one of: `existing_relationship`, `documented_opt_in`, `relevant_b2b_inquiry_to_published_company_inbox` (counsel to confirm), `do_not_send`.

## Quality bar

If a row has `[COMPANY_EMAIL]`, **do not guess** `info@domain`. Guessing bounces and looks like scraping. Call or use the contact form, then log the real address with `source_url`.
