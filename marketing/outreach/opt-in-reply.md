# Opt-in reply templates (`founder@verdansc.com`)

**These are replies only.** Paste them when someone already wrote in — a Craigslist reply, a Facebook comment or inbound DM, a site contact, or another message they started.

**Do not** send these as a blast. **Do not** scrape Facebook groups, Zillow, or Redfin for emails. **Do not** harvest “found emails.” This file is not a prospect list.

**From / reply-to:** `founder@verdansc.com`  
**This repo does not send mail.**

Cold / commercial blasts stay blocked until there is a real postal address (PO box or registered agent) **and** a working unsubscribe page. See `marketing/schedule/email-gate.md`.

Craigslist posts are not email and do not need an unsubscribe URL.
