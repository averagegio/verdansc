## Reply snippets

### “Is this Zillow / Apartments.com?”

No. Verdansc is a service portal: credit check, rental applications, and landlord intake links. Listings appear when a landlord creates an intake — we’re not claiming a full ABQ catalog.

### “Does the credit check hurt my score?”

The live credit-check page is a **$19 one-time soft inquiry**. The product states it does not affect your credit score. You enter details, then pay on Stripe’s checkout (card is not typed on Verdansc).

### “I’m a landlord with one duplex in Rio Rancho. Is this overkill?”

You can create a single intake link (title, address, fee, requirements) and share it. Membership is optional. Landlord Growth is there if you screen regularly; you don’t have to subscribe just to look.

### “Can my renters apply from their phone?”

Yes. You share `/apply/…` from intake setup. They save a draft, pay your application fee, and you see paid submissions on the dashboard.

### “Do you upload listing photos / MLS photos?”

Not as a listing gallery today. Landlords set listings up with title, address, fee, and requirements. Photos are for move-in / move-out documentation. 3D tours can be requested as a marketing service.

### “Is this only Albuquerque?”

The site isn’t locked to one city. This invite is for ABQ / Rio Rancho groups because that’s who we’re talking to here. Use a real street address on your listing.

### “How much?”

- Credit check: $19 one-time
- Renter membership: $15/mo (Club) or $29/mo (Plus)
- Landlord membership: $99/mo (Growth) or $249/mo (Pro)
- Application fees: set by each landlord

Pricing: https://www.verdansc.com/pricing?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=abq_invite&utm_content=reply

### “Can you screen this person for me / pull their credit if I send a name?”

No. We don’t take names from Facebook. The applicant runs their own check or applies through your intake link. Please don’t send SSNs or IDs in DMs.
