# Verdansc Facebook group invitations

Copy-only kit for inviting **Albuquerque / Rio Rancho** Facebook groups to try [Verdansc](https://verdansc.com). These files are drafts for a human to post. **Do not treat this as proof that anything was posted to Facebook.**

Primary CTA domain: `https://verdansc.com`  
Brand: **VERDANSC** (wordmark) / Verdansc in sentences  
Slogan (use sparingly): “Real estate, mapped to action.”

Prefer a **value post** (what the tool actually does, who it helps, one link) over a hard sell. If a group only allows discussion, skip the post and answer a relevant question with a short, disclosed reply instead.

## Posting etiquette (required)

1. **Read each group’s rules** before posting. Honor approval queues, “no promo” days, and intro-only threads.
2. **Do not spam.** One thoughtful post per group. Do not cross-post the same text to every group in the same hour. Do not bump with “following.”
3. **Disclose affiliation** in the post or the first comment. Example: “I’m with Verdansc.”
4. **Prefer value.** Lead with a concrete local workflow (screening, applications, intake links), not a slogan.
5. **Do not scrape or harvest.** Never copy member names, emails, or phone numbers from a group. Do not cold-DM people who did not comment or message first.
6. **Do not invent inventory.** Verdansc listings come from landlords who create intake links. Do not claim we have a full ABQ/Rio Rancho rental catalog.
7. **Do not fake scarcity** (“only 3 spots,” “today only”). Membership and the $19 credit check are published prices, not flash deals.
8. **Fair housing.** Do not write copy that steers, excludes, or screens by protected class. Point landlords to their own compliance duties.
9. **Credit-check accuracy.** The live product is a **$19 one-time soft inquiry** that the site states **does not affect credit score**. Do not call it a hard pull, a full tri-merge, or “instant approval.”
10. **Request-only services.** 3D home tours, draft agreements, escrow, and broker matching are **request / follow-up** surfaces, not self-serve checkout. Mention them only as “you can request,” or omit them.

## Product facts this kit is allowed to claim

Verified against the live app routes (see `utm-links.md`):

**For renters / buyers**

- Browse rental application listings and compare application fees (`/listings`)
- Apply to a listing, save a draft, pay the landlord’s application fee, track status (`/apply/[listingId]`)
- Run a $19 soft credit check with Stripe checkout (`/credit-check`)
- Optional membership: Rental Ready Club ($15/mo) or Rental Ready Plus ($29/mo) (`/signup?role=renter`, `/pricing`)
- Move-in planner with optional photo evidence for condition / utilities / insurance (`/move-in-planner`) — membership-oriented workflow

**For landlords / managers**

- Create an applicant intake listing (title, address, application fee, requirements) and share an apply link (`/rental-application`)
- Review paid applications in the dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- Optional membership: Landlord Growth ($99/mo) or Landlord Pro ($249/mo) (`/signup?role=landlord`)
- Move-out tracker with optional photo evidence for notice / condition / files (`/move-out-tracker`)

**Not live as self-serve listing photo galleries.** Do not say landlords “upload listing photos to go live on a map.” Photos in-product today are **evidence uploads** on move-in / move-out workflows. 3D tours are a requestable marketing service (`/3d-home-tour`).

**Local positioning:** The product is not geo-locked, but this GTM kit is written for Albuquerque and Rio Rancho groups. Invite locals to use the tools. Do not claim a brick-and-mortar office, exclusive ABQ inventory, or scraped local leads.

## Brand voice

- Professional and neighborly, not hype.
- Concrete verbs: browse, apply, screen, share an intake link, run a soft check.
- Dual-sided: renters bring applications; landlords bring listings.
- Map-first product language is fine in longer posts (“service map”); keep short posts in plain English.
- Company name: **Verdansc**. All-caps **VERDANSC** is the product wordmark; use it in images, not in body sentences.
