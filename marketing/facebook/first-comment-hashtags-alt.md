# First comment, hashtags, and image alt-text

Supporting copy only. **This kit does not mean a Facebook post was published.**

## Suggested first comment

Copy from `replies-and-dms.md`, or this shorter variant:

Affiliation note: I’m with Verdansc. Concrete links (same as the post):

Renters → https://verdansc.com/credit-check?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=abq_invite&utm_content=first_comment
Listings → https://verdansc.com/listings?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=abq_invite&utm_content=first_comment
Landlords → https://verdansc.com/rental-application?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=abq_invite&utm_content=first_comment

## Hashtags (sparing)

Facebook groups often ignore or penalize hashtag stacks. Use **zero or one**. If the group already uses a city tag, match theirs.

Allowed examples (pick at most one):

- `#Albuquerque`
- `#RioRancho`
- `#ABQ`

Do not use `#1`, `#limitedtime`, `#passiveincome`, `#creditrepair`, or competitor-bait tags. Do not hashtag protected-class housing terms.

## Image alt-text ideas

If you attach a graphic, write alt-text that describes the image and stays true to the product. Suggested lines:

1. **Map wordmark:** “VERDANSC wordmark over a dark map-style background. Text: Intelligent Real Estate API Map. verdansc.com.”
2. **Renter path:** “Simple graphic listing three renter steps: browse listings, $19 soft credit check, apply online. Albuquerque and Rio Rancho noted as the local invite, not as a full listing catalog.”
3. **Landlord path:** “Simple graphic listing three owner steps: create applicant intake, share an apply link, review paid applications. No claim of MLS photo upload.”
4. **Price card (only if the image shows prices):** “Price card: credit check $19 one-time; Rental Ready Club $15 per month; Landlord Growth $99 per month. Source: Verdansc pricing page.”

Do not put “Approved!” seals, fake occupancy counts, or photos of real homes you do not have rights to use.
