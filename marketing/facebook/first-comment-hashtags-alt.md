## Hashtags (sparing)

Facebook groups often ignore or penalize hashtag stacks. Use **zero or one**. If the group already uses a city tag, match theirs.

Allowed examples (pick at most one):

- `#Albuquerque`
- `#RioRancho`
- `#ABQ`

Do not use `#1`, `#limitedtime`, `#passiveincome`, `#creditrepair`, or competitor-bait tags. Do not hashtag protected-class housing terms.
