Hi neighbors — I’m with Verdansc. If you own a rental in Albuquerque or Rio Rancho (or you’re helping a family member lease one), this is a practical workflow, not a “download our app” blast.

A lot of small owners still juggle texts, paper applications, and a separate credit site. Verdansc puts intake, applications, and a paid soft credit check in one place.

**For property owners and small managers**

- Create a listing intake: title, street address, the application fee you charge, and optional requirements (income notes, move-in timing)
- Copy one apply link and send it to people who already inquired — Facebook comments, email, or a yard-sign QR code later
- Paid applications land in your dashboard so you’re not hunting for PDFs
- Point serious applicants to a **$19 soft credit check** (soft inquiry; the product copy says it does not affect their score)
- Membership is optional: Landlord Growth is $99/month if you screen often; you can also look around first

**If you share this with renters**

- Join or subscribe first at https://www.verdansc.com (sign up: `/signup`)
- Then get credit-ready with the $19 soft check and apply from listings
- Browse listings and see the fee before applying
- Apply online, save a draft, then pay the fee to submit
- Optional Rental Ready Club at $15/month if they apply more than once

**Photos, honestly**

You can attach photos on move-in and move-out workflows (condition, utilities, notice files). That is documentation, not a listing photo gallery. If you want walkthrough-style marketing media, you can request 3D home tours on the site — that’s a follow-up service, not an instant upload.
