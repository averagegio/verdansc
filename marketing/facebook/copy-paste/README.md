## How to paste (each group)

1. Confirm you are a **member** of that group and read its rules (promo, video, links in comments). Skip if you are not a member or the rules forbid this.
2. Open that group’s composer. **One post per group.** Do not blast all seven in the same hour.
3. Attach the still **before** you paste: `marketing/ads/exports/verdansc-split-ad-4x5.jpg` (preferred) or `verdansc-split-ad-4x5.png`.
4. Paste **alt text**, then the **post text**.
5. Optional: attach `marketing/ads/exports/verdansc-split-ad-30s-9x16.mp4` **only if that group allows video / Reels**. If unsure, still only.
6. Publish, then immediately paste the **first comment** (pitch + graphic honesty).
7. Do **not** DM anyone. Do **not** copy member names or harvest contacts.
