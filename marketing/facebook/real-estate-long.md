Hi everyone — I’m with Verdansc, and I wanted to share a local tool rather than a pitch deck.

Verdansc is a map-first rental service portal. For Albuquerque and Rio Rancho agents, landlords, and renters, the live workflows today are screening, applications, and listing intake — not a promise of every house in the metro already sitting on our map.

**If you work with renters or buyers**

- Browse active rental application listings and compare the application fee before you apply
- Submit an application from a landlord’s shareable link (draft first, then pay the fee to send it)
- Run a **$19 one-time soft credit check** (the site states this does not affect the credit score)
- Optional membership if you apply often: Rental Ready Club at $15/month

**If you list or manage property**

- Set up applicant intake: property title, address, your application fee, and any requirements
- Share one apply link; paid applications show up in your dashboard for review
- Optional membership: Landlord Growth at $99/month for intake links and member-rate credit-check volume
- Move-out tracker if you need notice, ledger, and photo documentation in one place

**What this is not**

We are not claiming a full ABQ/Rio Rancho catalog, and we are not collecting group member data. 3D tours, draft agreements, escrow, and broker matching can be requested on the site; they are not self-serve checkout yet.
